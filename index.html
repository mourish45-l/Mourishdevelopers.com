Payment and Deployment

Domain & Service Maintenance

Annual Renewals: Domain names and hosting services require yearly payment.

Payment Options: You may choose to pay the registrar directly or send the payment to us to process on your behalf.

Late Renewal Risk: Failure to pay the yearly maintenance fee may result in the loss of your domain name and website accessibility.

Data Deletion: Once the setup is complete (e.g., Firebase is linked or the domain is secured), we will permanently remove your accounts and sensitive documents from our devices.
